HOW THIS EXAMPLE WAS DECIDED

Read it against the input beside it. Its subject is a working harbour ferry.
Yours is a superyacht. Copy the reasoning, never the vessel or the
sentences.

WHAT WAS SET ASIDE FROM THE INSPIRATION

Everything that made the source ferry's design its own was set aside: its
length, beam and passenger count, the aluminium catamaran hull, the
enclosed saloon and its bar, and the open upper deck at the stern. None of
them appears in the answer, renamed or otherwise. Only one abstract
question was kept: how can each stop take less of the crossing?

ONE IDEA, THE WHOLE VESSEL

central_idea states one rule: people move on and off across the ferry's
width. The identical ends, the gates along both sides, the benches across
the width and the helm cabin at mid-length all follow from it, and none of
them is a source feature turned the other way round.

THE REQUIREMENT AND THE MOVING PART

The profile asked for sides that transform. The answer designs the gates
in each state: what they slide on, how they sit closed, what they are
open, where they stack and the band they sweep. A design without such a
requirement need not move at all.

THE PROFILE DETAILS THE SAME DESIGN

The protagonist profile adds detail and contradicts nothing in the thesis.
form_and_proportions gives the hull in profile and in plan before the
roof and helm cabin it carries. figures inherits length and beam exactly
from principal_dimensions; the other numbers are proposed or left
undetermined. The roof, a focus region of this profile, has its own
signature feature. The one brief space, the passenger hall, has one
interior entry with its deck, neighbours, ways in and what makes it
recognizable.

THE SAME DESIGN AS STRUCTURED GEOMETRY

canonical_design restates the ferry as ids, relations and states, and
adds nothing the profile does not describe. The single end door is
transformed into the gate band and the end wheelhouse is rejected; both
patterns come from anti_regression and are forbidden only because they
conflict with this design. superyacht_reading, written here for a ferry,
says how the whole still reads as its own kind of vessel. Every mass and
void takes part in a relationship, every signature region has a
transition, and both transforming features have configuration states
while the fixed helm cabin has none. The core sections, both masses, the
hall void and both P0 regions are locked, and every locked relationship,
transition, invariant and proof names only locked parts. What the profile
calls a proposal is proposed: the end decks therefore sit in the
secondary geometry rather than among the primary voids, and they, the
walkway, the rail and the finishes stay out of every invariant. Each P0
region and each P0 must_preserve entry names the views that prove it.

WHAT THIS STEP DOES NOT DO

There is no premise, no stage, no event and no scene. The film is written
later, around this design, and may not change it.
