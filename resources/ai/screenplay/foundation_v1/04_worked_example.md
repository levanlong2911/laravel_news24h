HOW THIS EXAMPLE WAS DECIDED

Read it against the input beside it. Its subject is a footbridge. Yours is
not. Copy the reasoning, never the crossing, the crews or the sentences.

WHAT THE THESIS DECIDED

central_idea states one rule: leave the floor open and protect the sides.
The other four fields are that rule seen from four sides, which is why they
can be checked against each other.

It does not claim the open deck performs better in wind than a solid one.
The input says the deck is open so wind passes through; the thesis repeats
that and stops. A thesis explaining why the edge members are also lighter
because of wind would be inventing engineering it cannot establish.

WHAT THE STAGE TREATMENTS ARE FOR

Each one says four things, and they are different questions.

dramatic_purpose is why the stage is in the film at all. Not what happens
in it, but what the film would lose without it. The construction entry says
the idea has to become metal with a fixed shape; that is a reason, not an
event.

observable_development is what a camera could record. It stays concrete
without becoming a scene: "a section is dragged clear for a wrong angle" is
observable, "the team maintains quality standards" is not.

participants names who is present, by role and as a group where the work is
collective. A fabrication crew is one participant, not six workers.

handover_to_next says what carries the film forward. This is the field that
stops a stage from being an island. The design entry ends with the
arrangement leaving the studio, and the construction entry begins from
parts that have arrived.

The final stage has nothing after it, so its handover_to_next says what the
film leaves the viewer with instead.

WHAT WAS LEFT OUT

The construction treatment says a crew reaches a joint and makes a
fastening. It never says how a section travels out over the gap or what
holds it on the way, because nothing in the input establishes that. Naming
an operation you cannot support is invention dressed as documentary; saying
less is honest.

WHAT THIS STEP DOES NOT DO

There are no scenes here, no scene count, no durations, no shot lists and
no dialogue. The synopsis tells the film as it unfolds, and the stage
treatments say what each stretch is for. Where the cuts fall is decided
later, by someone reading this.

Five stage treatments appear because the profile lists five stages. That is
the profile's order, not a target for anything else.
