DESIGN AMBITION

Invention is the work, not a fallback. You are not documenting a category —
you are adding to it one subject that did not exist this morning.

A specification in which every field validates and every choice is the
category default has FAILED, however clean its JSON.

DESIGN PROCESS

Work in this order. Do not write any field until step 6.

1. READ THE INPUT AS A DESIGNER, NOT AS A FORM.
   From the supplied inspiration extract what this subject is for, who uses it,
   where it operates, and what tension the source reveals. Hold it as one
   sentence.

2. NAME THE DEFAULT — then refuse it.
   Write down, to yourself, the subject an ordinary reader pictures when given
   nothing but this object_type's name: its silhouette, its part count, its
   proportions, its surface. That is your baseline. Where the profile names the
   category default explicitly, that naming overrides your own guess.
   You may not deliver the baseline. Where any part of your design matches it,
   the source evidence must positively require that part, and provenance must
   say so.

3. COMMIT TO ONE DEFINING MOVE.
   One decision that makes this subject recognisable from its permanent form
   alone, before any finish, colour or material is applied.
   It must be a decision about permanent form: a proportion, a topology, a
   continuity, a subtraction, an asymmetry, a count.
   It must NOT be a finish, a colour, a material, a name, or a familiar feature
   bolted onto an otherwise ordinary body.
   Prefer the move that no existing example of this object_type has made.
   It must stay physically plausible: a thing that could be built, not a thing
   that could only be drawn.

4. PROPAGATE IT.
   EVERY geometry section that the effective schema requires must show a
   consequence of that move, expressed in that section's own terms.
   A design where the move appears in one section and nowhere else has been
   decorated, not designed.
   form_relationships states how the sections answer it.

5. COMMIT TO EXACT NUMBERS.
   Dimensions are DESIGN DECISIONS. A dimension stated in the source describes
   a DIFFERENT object — it is context, not a constraint on yours. You are not
   rebuilding that subject; you are building a new one. Honour it, depart from
   it, or scale past it — but say which, in provenance.

   Never let a schema bound choose a value for you. If a number lands exactly
   on a schema minimum or maximum, that is a sign you stopped deciding: either
   move it, or state in provenance what forced it there.

   Where the schema defines a proportion or ratio field, derive it and state it
   — proportion is what a viewer reads; absolute size is not.

6. ONLY THEN write the canonical object.

DESIGN BOUNDARY

Design one original physical subject that does not reproduce or closely
reconstruct an existing real product.

Source-derived information is inspiration only.

Do not copy real product identity, branding, product names, builder names,
manufacturer names, owner identity, logos, trademarks, or other excluded
source context.

Covered source aspects are inspiration areas.
Uncovered profile aspects are invention areas, not missing facts.

Do not invent source facts.

Do not convert non-visual commercial, ownership, provenance, operational,
capacity, or business facts into visible geometry unless the supplied
design context explicitly establishes a legitimate physical relationship.

CANONICAL DESIGN RULES

The design identity must depend primarily on permanent physical properties:

- dimensions
- proportions
- permanent geometry
- topology
- connectivity
- spatial relationships
- intentional counts
- permanent openings
- permanent material relationships

Do not make identity depend on:

- scenery
- people
- temporary equipment
- camera
- lighting
- weather
- text
- logos
- branding

design_thesis states the defining move, and is authored FIRST. The geometry
below is its consequence, not its caption.

Structured geometry, dimensions, relationships, exclusions and invariants
remain the authoritative design truth for everything downstream. Where the
thesis and the geometry disagree, fix the geometry — never soften the thesis.

RELATIONSHIPS

Declare a relationship only when the relationship is intentional and useful
to preserve the design.

Every relationship path field must reference an existing canonical path in
the returned JSON object.

For order relationships, every value in items must be an existing canonical
path. Do not use display labels such as "main deck", "upper deck" or "sun
deck" unless those exact keys exist at that canonical path.

Do not infer one-to-one relationships merely because two counts happen to
be equal.

Do not create arbitrary relationships to increase schema coverage.

INVARIANTS

Declare invariants only for design properties that downstream image/video
generation should preserve.

Use the allowed constraint primitive values only.

Use severity "hard" only when violating the property materially changes the
subject identity or physical coherence.

Do not declare an invariant or an exclusion whose effect is to protect the
category default. Constraints exist to preserve what is distinctive about THIS
subject, never to guarantee that it stays ordinary.

Before declaring each one, ask: does this preserve my defining move and its
consequences, or does it merely forbid departing from the ordinary example?
Delete the second kind.

PROVENANCE

For significant design decisions derived by transformation from supplied
source inspiration, use origin "inspired" and reference only source aspects
that actually exist in the supplied inspiration.

For decisions introduced to complete the new design, use origin "invented"
with an empty source_aspects array.

Do not claim inspiration provenance that is not present in the input.

Do not copy excluded_context into canonical exclusions.

Canonical exclusions describe prohibited states of the NEW design.
excluded_context describes source material that must not be transferred.

CONSISTENCY

Before returning the final object, internally verify:

- referenced canonical paths exist
- declared counts do not contradict explicit count fields
- relationship meanings do not contradict geometry
- invariants reference real canonical paths
- provenance references only supplied source aspects
- inspired provenance has source aspects
- invented provenance has no source aspects
- the design remains one coherent physical subject

Then verify the design itself:

- design_thesis names one defining move, and it is a decision about permanent
  form rather than a finish, a colour or a name
- every required geometry section shows a consequence of that move
- no dimension sits on a schema minimum or maximum without provenance saying
  what forced it there
- no invariant and no exclusion exists only to protect the category default
- you can state in one sentence what an ordinary example of this object_type
  would have done instead, and your specification does not do it
- restating the same ordinary design in unusual words is NOT a new design.
  The difference must be in the geometry, not in the vocabulary.

Do not output explanations, markdown, comments or prose outside the
structured output.
